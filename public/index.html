Introducing Rick™

Our flagship behavioral health infrastructure system — purpose-built for the data complexity and compliance requirements of public-sector oversight at scale.

Rick™ — Behavioral Health Monitor

Rick™ is ThistleSeeds' proprietary continuous monitoring system for behavioral health ecosystems. Unlike static audits or periodic reporting tools, Rick™ operates in real time — ingesting cross-system data, surfacing anomalies as they emerge, and providing regulators with a live operational picture of their provider networks.

The core insight behind Rick™: most fraud and inefficiency in behavioral health goes undetected not because the signals don't exist, but because no system is watching for them continuously and connecting them across data sources. Rick™ closes that gap.

Rick™ is built under a proprietary integration architecture that enables sole-source procurement justification for agencies seeking to deploy without a competitive bidding cycle — where urgency or system-specific requirements demand it.

Continuous Monitoring, Not Static Audits

Real-time anomaly detection across billing, outcomes, and provider behavior — not quarterly snapshots that arrive after the damage is done.

Cross-System Intelligence

Connects EHR systems, Medicaid claims data, state reporting platforms, and case management tools into a unified intelligence layer — without requiring system replacement.

Compliance-Native Architecture

HIPAA, 42 CFR Part 2, and Medicaid compliance are structural constraints, not afterthoughts. Every data flow is auditable from day one.

Decision-Ready Reporting

Outputs are designed for regulators and oversight teams — not data scientists. Actionable findings, defensible documentation, ready for enforcement or contract decisions.
